MEXICO READY LIFTS - standalone app

Files: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png
Keep all six files together in one folder.

PUT IT ONLINE (free, one time, ~10 min, easiest on a computer)
1. Make a free account at github.com
2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it: lifts. Set it to Public. Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in all six files, click Commit changes.
4. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/lifts/

ON YOUR IPHONE
1. Open that link in Safari.
2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
3. Open it from the Home Screen icon from now on (works offline, full screen).

BACK UP WEEKLY
Settings (gear) > Save backup file > Save to Files (iCloud Drive).
New phone or wiped data: Settings > Restore from backup file.
