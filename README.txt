MEXICO READY LIFTS - update (weekly progress charts, exercise history in workouts, redrawn diagrams)

TO UPDATE YOUR APP ON GITHUB
1. Open github.com/kihwankimm-spec/Fitness
2. Add file > Upload files
3. Drag in index.html (same name, it replaces the old one)
4. Commit changes
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. On your iPhone, open the app, close it fully, open it again.

Your logged workouts are not touched by updating.
